What I don’t do

If you’ve ever had a cold call promising to “get you to the top of Google”, read this.

  • I don’t ask for passwords. You give me manager access and can remove it whenever you like.
  • I don’t promise the top spot on Google. Nobody can, and anyone who does is selling you smoke.
  • I don’t touch your till or your booking system. What works stays as it is.
  • I don’t buy reviews or write fake ones. Google catches them, and your listing pays the price.
  • I don’t change your listing’s name or address without talking to you. Google can ask you to verify it again, or suspend it.
